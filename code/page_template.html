Darkest skies

Where can you still see the Milky Way? A map of the glow our lights throw into the night sky, for the whole world and for every square kilometre of the UK, built from satellite images of the Earth at night and checked against {{model.n_fit|int}} readings from hand-held sky meters.

    Click the map or search a place to read its sky.
    Sky glow is modelled from VIIRS annual night lights for 2023 (world at 2.5 arc minutes, about 4.6 km; UK and Ireland at 30 arc seconds, about 1 km), as described below. It is artificial light only, at the zenith, on a clear moonless night. The UK change layer compares the model run with 2015 and 2023 lights. Base map tiles © Esri.
